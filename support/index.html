Support

Stuck, found a bug or have an idea? Check the questions below, or write to hello@kulhadgames.com.

When you write, please tell us: your phone model, Android or iOS version, the game version (Settings → More, at the bottom) and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

Playing

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down (hold) to slide. Tap villagers to high-five them, and tap Sheru's button when his meter is full. Bring home bricks, clay and bamboo to rebuild Sonpur in the village screen.

The game is slow or stutters on my phone.

The game picks a graphics level for your phone automatically. In Settings → Game → Graphics you can choose "Battery saver 30" for a steadier frame rate. Closing other apps and letting a hot phone cool down also helps. The first run after installing can take a moment while the game prepares its graphics.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game works without the internet. Videos, cloud save and purchases need a connection.

How do I switch between Hindi and English?

Settings → Game → Language. Settings → Access has larger text, colour-blind cues, reduced motion and a left-handed layout.

How do I turn reminders or vibration on or off?

Settings → Game. Reminders are off until you turn them on and come at most once a day.

Progress

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Turn on cloud save (Settings → More) on your old phone and let it sync. On the new phone, sign in to the same Google Play Games or iCloud account and open the game. It keeps whichever copy has more progress.

My progress is gone after reinstalling.

Without cloud save, progress lives only on the phone and is removed when the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared. We don't keep a copy on our servers, so we can't restore it.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Settings → More → Reset progress. Everything except your purchases is erased.

Purchases and ads

I bought something but didn't get it.

Make sure you're online, then open the Dukaan → ₹ Store tab. On iPhone, tap "Restore purchases". If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Google Play (play.google.com → Order history) or Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

Why does the game ask a maths question before buying?

That's the grown-up check, so that children don't buy things by accident.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Videos are always optional. You choose to watch one for a reward, like a revive or double materials. "Remove Ads" in the store removes the between-run ads; optional reward videos stay available.
