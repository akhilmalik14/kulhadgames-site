Privacy policy

Gaon Ki Daud · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Applies to version 0.9.0 and later

Gaon Ki Daud ("the game", "we", "us") is made by Akhil Malik, trading as Kulhad Games, India. Contact: hello@kulhadgames.com. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • The game works offline. Your progress is saved on your phone. If you turn on cloud save, a copy is kept in your own Google Play Games or iCloud account, not on our servers.
  • We collect anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to fix bugs and balance the game.
  • Videos are always optional. They are shown by an ad network that may use your device's advertising ID, but only for players who said they are 18 or older.
  • We do not sell personal data.

1. What is collected

1.1 Gameplay statistics (analytics)

The game sends gameplay events through Google Firebase Analytics, together with an app-instance ID that Firebase creates for this installation. It is not your name or phone number. The events are:

  • sessions: when a session starts and ends (time spent in the game), and whether you came back on day 1, 3 or 7;
  • runs: mode, distance, seconds, zone, weather, coins (paise), score, revives and how the run ended; errand results;
  • progress: tasks done, potli earned and opened, building stages, the first-play tutorial steps and unlocks;
  • shop and ads: purchase started, completed, cancelled, failed or restored (product id and price tier, never payment details); rewarded video started, rewarded or not available;
  • settings changed (for example language or larger text), reminders switched on, progress reset, the cloud sync result and taps on the share button;
  • three properties: the chosen hero (Gudiya or Chhotu), the player level and the age band ("under 18" or "18+", see section 5).

Firebase also receives basic device information it needs to work: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, country (from the network) and the app-instance ID. A copy of the same events is written to a file on your phone for debugging; it never leaves the phone by itself.

1.2 Crash reports

Google Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports and error messages: the stack trace, device model, operating-system version, app version, free memory and the app-instance ID. The game also keeps a short error log on the phone.

1.3 Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

Rewarded videos are optional: you tap "watch a video" to get a reward such as a revive. They are never offered in your first two sessions. The game may also show an occasional full-screen ad when you return from a finished run (this is currently switched off). Such ads are never shown during play, never in your first three days, and never to players who bought "Remove Ads".

Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, a Unity company) and its partner ad networks. For players who said they are 18 or older, they may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and app information to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

For players under 18, or who skipped the age question, ads are requested as non-personalised, the advertising ID is not collected and no behavioural targeting is used.

On iPhone the game does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it or its ad partners. You can reset or delete the advertising ID on Android at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording differs between phones).

1.4 In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive a purchase confirmation (product id, transaction id) so we can deliver the item and restore it later. We never see your card, UPI or other payment details. Purchases are saved with your game progress. Every purchase first asks a grown-up question (a multiplication sum).

1.5 Cloud save (optional)

If you use cloud save, your progress file is stored under your own Google or Apple account: in Google Play Games saved games on Android, or iCloud key-value storage on iPhone. The file holds game progress only (paise, materials, buildings, stickers and game settings), no personal data. Google and Apple keep it under their own privacy policies; we cannot read it on a server.

1.6 Reminders (optional, off by default)

If you turn reminders on, your phone schedules at most one local reminder a day. Nothing is sent to us.

1.7 Share cards

When you tap Share, the game draws a picture of your run or village and hands it to your phone's share sheet. We do not upload or keep it.

1.8 What we never collect

Name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location from GPS, microphone, camera, messages, files or browsing history.

2. Why we use it

To run the game, fix crashes, understand which parts are fun or too hard (balance), show optional videos, deliver and restore purchases, and keep your progress safe. We do not use your data to make automated decisions about you.

4. How long it is kept

  • Analytics events: up to 14 months in Firebase, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: 90 days in Crashlytics.
  • Files on your phone (progress, the local event log and the error log): until you uninstall the game or use Settings → More → Reset progress.
  • Cloud save: until you delete it from your Google Play Games or iCloud account.

5. Age and young players

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not designed for children and is not part of Google Play's Families programme.

At first launch the game asks for your year of birth (you can skip it). It keeps only an age band on the phone, "under 18" or "18+", never the year itself. You can change it in Settings → More → Age group.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act treats everyone under 18 as a child. So for players under 18, or who skip the question:

  • ads are non-personalised;
  • the advertising ID is not collected;
  • analytics run without an advertising ID;
  • no behavioural targeting is used.

Purchases are behind a grown-up check. Parents can write to hello@kulhadgames.com to ask us to delete analytics data linked to their child's installation.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete progress on the phone: Settings → More → Reset progress, or uninstall the game.
  • Delete analytics and crash data: write to hello@kulhadgames.com with the approximate date you installed the game and your phone model. We will find and delete the matching data with Firebase within 30 days. Step-by-step: Delete your data.
  • Turn off personalised ads: set your age group to under 18 in the game, or reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone settings.
  • Your rights: if you are in India (DPDP Act 2023), the EU or UK (GDPR) or another region with privacy laws, you may ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent, by writing to hello@kulhadgames.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

7. Security

Data the game sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS). Your save is stored in the game's private folder on the phone.
