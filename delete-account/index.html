Delete your Gaon Ki Daud data

Gaon Ki Daud by Kulhad Games (Akhil Malik) · Android package com.kulhadgames.gaonkidaud

Gaon Ki Daud has no accounts. You never sign up, so there is no account to close. Your game data lives in up to three places. Here is how to delete each one.

1. Progress on your phone

  • In the game: Settings (the gear in the village's top bar) → More → Reset progress → confirm. Your progress, paise, materials, buildings and stickers are erased at once; purchases you paid for are kept so you don't lose them.
  • Everything, including purchases on this phone: uninstall the game, or on Android go to Settings → Apps → Gaon Ki Daud → Storage → Clear storage. Paid items can be restored later from the store.

2. Cloud save (only if you turned it on)

Reset progress also replaces the cloud copy the next time the game syncs. To remove the cloud copy itself:

  • Android, Google Play Games: open the Google Play Games app → your profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, where you can delete the data of this game only.
  • iPhone, iCloud: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. If Gaon Ki Daud is listed there, delete its data. If it isn't listed, write to us and we'll explain the remaining steps.

3. Analytics and crash data we hold

The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, linked to a random ID for your installation (see the privacy policy). To have them deleted:

  1. Email hello@kulhadgames.com with the subject "Delete my Gaon Ki Daud data".
  2. Tell us roughly when you installed the game, your phone model, and whether it's Android or iPhone, so we can find your installation's data.
  3. We delete the matching analytics and crash data in Firebase within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Data that isn't deleted on request is removed automatically anyway: analytics after at most 14 months, and crash reports after 90 days.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. Google Play and Apple keep their own records of purchases (order history) under their policies; we can't delete those. Ad networks' data about adult players' advertising IDs is controlled by them. Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and see Unity's privacy policy.
