How we make games

Fair play. Videos are always optional and nothing you can buy is pay-to-win. Everything in the shop can also be earned by playing.

Made for everyday phones. Our games are tuned to run smoothly on 3 GB Android phones and to work offline.

Hindi and English. Hindi comes first, with English always there too, plus larger text, colour-blind cues, reduced motion and a left-handed layout.

Respectful. Animals are never harmed, and we keep personal data to a minimum. Read our privacy policy.
